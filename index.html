How it works

Three steps, twenty minutes

    Type them in, or import a CSV with name,table,tags. The bad rows come back with a line number instead of silently disappearing.

  2. Seat them

  3. Print and share

    Download the A2 PDF for the print shop and the QR sheet for the reception door. Guests scan, type their name, see their table.

Questions

Before you buy

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log into. The chart lives in your browser and the purchase unlocks downloads on that device.

Where do my guests’ names go?

Nowhere. The app runs entirely in your browser and does not send your list to a server. You can even open the file with no internet connection.

What can my print shop do with the file?

The PDF is A2 portrait with the fonts embedded, so it prints correctly anywhere. Ask for 200 gsm matte or a foam board if it will stand on an easel.

Can I edit it after I pay?

Yes. The seating is yours to change as RSVPs come in, and you can re-download the files as often as you need.

How does the QR code work?

The sheet contains a link to your guest search page. A guest scans it, types their name and sees their table — or “not seated yet” if they are still on your list.
